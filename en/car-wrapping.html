Services

Everything for a flawless car

From a full colour change to invisible paint protection — every project is executed with precision and quality materials.

01

Full wrap

Completely transform your car: matte, gloss, satin or custom colours. Quality film with precise installation that also protects the original paint and makes you stand out on the road. We use premium materials: 3М, Avery Dennison, Oracal, XPEL, SunTek.

  • Matte / Gloss / Satin
  • Custom colours
  • Paint protection

02

PPF — paint protection

Transparent PPF protects the paint from stone chips, scratches, road dirt and daily wear. High-stress areas — bumper, hood, fenders, sills — keep their look longer. A good choice for new and sports cars.

  • Anti-chip
  • Self-healing
  • Gloss / Matte

03

Custom Details

Partial wrapping and custom details for precise accents. Roof, mirrors, hood, trim or special design elements — we develop solutions tailored to your car and style.

  • Roof / Mirrors

  • Design accents

Vehicle branding

Window Tinting & Privacy
